    TECHNIQUES

    • Ground string: Slash ×4. Slash, Slash, Heavy launches.
    • Juggle: after the launcher hits, Jump, then Slash ×3, then Heavy to slam.
    • Parry: tap Guard just before a hit lands. The attacker reels and you can Counter with Slash.
    • Afterimage: dodge at the last moment and the world slows to a third. Slash to Counter. (3 s cooldown)
    • Red glint: the thrust that follows can't be blocked or parried. Dodge it.
    • Posture: fill the enemy's white bar to break them, then Heavy for Lantern Break. It cuts through any recovery.
    • Dash strike: Slash straight out of a dodge.
    • In the air: Jump again for a double jump; Dodge for an air dash that homes in on your target. Both refresh when you land.
    • Signature chain: Slash, Slash, Gale, Jump, Slash, Slash, Dodge, Slash, Heavy.
    • Vacuum Pull drags enemies to your blade, even up into the air. Tempest Edge spins three hits all around you.
    • Mana refills a little with every sword hit.
    • Surge (the bright bar) fills as you deal and take damage, parry, and dodge perfectly. When it's full, unleash Skyrender.
    • Boss: Hask the Bogwarden. When it dives, a mud mound hunts you and mud waves skim the ground: jump or dodge them, or you'll be WEIGHTED (slow, low jumps; Juno's Stitch cleans it off). Hit the mound with a wind spell to UPROOT it, launched and stunned. A red circle means it's about to erupt beneath you. At 65% it calls Fen Hounds; at 35% the bog drains, it stops diving, and its exposed underside takes 50% more damage.
    • Know your enemy: Fen Hounds rush in packs and lunge from range. Cantors sing violet bolts (slash them out of the air, parry, or dodge) and a Hymn that wards their allies: hit a warded foe with wind to Dispel it. Bulwarks block everything from the front: circle behind, guard-break them with heavies, or have Juno bind them. The Bog Beast ★ can't be launched or interrupted until you break its posture, and its red-glint mud slam can't be blocked.
    • Your squad: Bas (Stone) and Juno (Thread) fight beside you. Call their Assists: Bas erupts a Pillar Uppercut under your target (it even re-launches juggled foes), Juno binds it with a Snare Line.
    • Stances: Press (hunt enemies), Guard (stay close, intercept attacks on you, Bas shields you), Support (hang back, Juno heals you).
    • Squad reactions: Snare an anchored foe = Taut Line (long stagger). Wind spell on a bound foe = Slingshot. Heavy hit on an anchored foe = Shatterstone (splash).
    • Marks: Launcher, the 4th Slash, Counter, and Skyrender's finish MARK a foe (gold ring). Hit a marked foe with any spell to Detonate it, splashing anyone close.
    • Assist (AST): wider parry and perfect-dodge windows, and holding Slash keeps the combo going on touch. On by default on touch screens.
